# Documentation

This directory contains the scientific and technical documentation produced throughout the development of the AquaRescue ROV project.

The documents preserve the project's original research context, methodology, development process and scientific dissemination.

## Scientific Article

### Embarcação Robótica de Reconhecimento e Localização de Vítimas de Afogamento

The original scientific article presents the development of a low-cost Remotely Operated Vehicle (ROV) designed to assist aquatic search and recovery operations.

The work focuses on improving the safety and efficiency of underwater search operations while exploring the reuse of electronic waste as a strategy for reducing development costs and increasing replicability. 

**Original document:**

[`artigofebrace.pdf`](./artigofebrace.pdf)

### Main topics

- Aquatic search and recovery
- Low-cost robotics
- Remotely Operated Vehicles (ROVs)
- Public safety
- Computer vision
- Embedded systems
- Reuse of electronic components
- Underwater robotics

The article describes the project as a low-cost ROV intended to make underwater search and recovery operations safer and more agile, reducing exposure of rescue professionals to hazardous aquatic environments.
## Project Evolution

The project continued to evolve after the original prototype.

Documentation from the later development stage records improvements to the flotation structure, hermetic enclosure, chassis, electronics and camera system, including improvements to image processing. 

The project was subsequently presented at scientific and technological events, including the Mostra Nacional de Robótica (MNR) and FEBRACE. The documented results include an ICJr scholarship award at MNR and a 4th-place national result in Engineering at FEBRACE, in addition to an award for Best Scientific Article.

## Research Context

The original research emphasizes the risks associated with conventional underwater rescue operations, particularly in environments with poor visibility, contamination, adverse weather and other operational hazards. 

The AquaRescue concept therefore follows a human-machine collaboration model: the robotic platform assists the rescue team while reducing the need for immediate human exposure to the underwater environment.

The original project documentation also describes future development toward improved camera quality and computer vision capabilities.
